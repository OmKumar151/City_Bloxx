## Description [4000]

Swing, drop, stack, and build the city of your dreams.

A crane swings each block over your tower. Tap at just the right moment to drop it. Land it squarely for a Perfect, chain Perfects together for bonus points, and watch your tower rise into the clouds. Land off-centre and the whole tower starts to sway, so steady hands win.

Every tower you finish becomes a building in your city. Place it on the map, grow your population, and unlock new building types and whole new cities.

FEATURES
• One-tap crane stacking that's easy to learn and hard to master
• Perfect landings, streak chimes and a tower that sways when you get sloppy
• Four building types with their own challenges
• A city map to plan and grow, with new cities to unlock
• Quick Play: endless stacking for your best score
• Satisfying haptic feedback on every landing
• Plays great on iPhone and iPad
• No account needed, and your progress stays on your device

How high can you build?
